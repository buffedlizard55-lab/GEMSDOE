What the numbers mean

  • DTI shaped > DTI raw on every run so far — the floor-plus-thinning step consistently helps, exactly as the metric algebra predicts.
  • FP_w dominates. The smoke model emits far too much diffuse mass. This is the single biggest lever available, and it is an output shaping problem as much as a modelling one.
  • Local scores are optimistic. They are computed against the labels used in training and are not the private test set.

Next steps, in priority order

  1. GPU training at full capacity — configs/config.yaml (UNet++/DeepLabV3+/SegFormer ensemble, EfficientNet-B5, 10 MC splits, 60 epochs, pretrained encoders). CPU runners cannot do this inside the job limit.
  2. Aggressive output thinning, guided by the measured dilation-cost curve on the Metric page.
  3. 1 m DEM derivatives from the 716 confirmed tiles — lineament and scarp signal at 100 m is the most direct physical evidence of surface faulting.
  4. Ensemble + TTA, already implemented, currently disabled in the smoke profile for time.
