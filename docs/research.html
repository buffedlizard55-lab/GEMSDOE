1. Why Faults = Geothermal — Scientific Basis

From About page https://www.drivendata.org/competitions/306/competition-doe-gems/page/968/#about-the-task: Fault = fracture with measurable displacement. Detected via field (scarps, offset, slickensides), seismic reflection, gravity/magnetic anomalies, remote sensing, elevation, edge detection, Hough, deep learning. Geothermal often in active/recent faulting where fractures are pathways for hot fluids. Faults act as conduits for fluids, concentrating minerals, hydrocarbons, geothermal, groundwater.

Verified tectonic context:

  • Walker Lane tectonic influences: Faulds & Henry 2008, AGS Digest 22:437-470 — PDF (fetched 2026-09-12)

2. GeoDAWN — High-Res Geophysics

Official: ScienceBase https://www.sciencebase.gov/catalog/item/657e1d85d34e23d3533209f7 DOI https://doi.org/10.5066/P93LGLVQ USGS Overview https://www.usgs.gov/data/geodawn-airborne-magnetic-and-radiometric-surveys-northwestern-great-basin-nevada-and

Purpose: high-res airborne magnetic & radiometric to support geologic mapping for geothermal & mineral, joint USGS Mineral Resource Program, EarthMRI, DOE GTO, plus lidar via 3DEP.

Provides: flight-line & gridded data, .grd/.map/.gdb readable with Oasis Montaj or free Geosoft Viewer https://www.seequent.com/products-solutions/geosoft-viewer/, shapefiles, .csv, GeoTIFFs, ternary maps. Files: 22103_area1_grids.zip 42MB, area2_grids 227MB, mag_a1_gdb 350MB, spec_a1_gdb 378MB, tiffs 43MB+230MB, 3GB contractor packages.

Physics: Magnetic anomalies reflect subsurface structure, radiometric K/Th/U surface geology. RTP corrects shift due to oblique field — canonical method Baranov & Naudy 1964 (see verified USGS OFR texts); the RTP band ships in the competition features. 4. Critical Thinking — Challenges & Biases

From Hermant et al 2025 discussion and problem page:

  • High imbalance: Few fault pixels vs background — need weights, focal params, adds hyperparameters requiring optimization, cannot be determined solely from stats
  • Complex signature: Faults similar to other geomorph objects (paleo-shoreline, scarp, canyon, stream) — most important issue
  • Label uncertainty: Manual maps never exhaustive, USGS incomplete, may contain inaccurate data (problem page)
  • Overfitting: Tendency to detect non-fault objects, need better discrimination via multi-source fusion
  • Spatial bias: Mapping robust in some sub-regions/states, insufficient in others — creates bias, need homogenize via model
  • Resolution: 100m training vs 1m DEM available — high-res reveals subtle scarps but resampling loses detail, need multi-scale
  • Physics: Faults cause elevation changes (SL increases), sharp changes/high gradients in mag images (edge/line detectors), gravity/mag inversion via CNN direct mapping without heavy priors
